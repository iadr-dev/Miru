14	## Visual design
15	Tokens/radius from [../design-system.md](../design-system.md) (card 16px, pad 20px). Cards `--surface`
16	+ `1px --border` + tinted shadow; 2-col grid ≥1280px.
17	
18	- **估值河流圖** — PER/PBR band ribbons (`--chart-1..3` at low alpha) with price line `--text` over;
19	  `--grid`/`--crosshair` per `ChartWithDataSheet`; tabular axis.
20	- **分析師深度** (4 cards) — 評等 distribution mini-bar (categorical `--chart-1..5`, **not** 紅漲綠跌) ·
21	  目標價 range + current mark · EPS/營收估計 bars · 升降評 timeline. Each card carries an **attribution
22	  line** ("N analysts · as of MM/DD", 13px `--text-muted`). **Coverage-gated → hidden when null**.
23	- **個股事件 timeline / 除權息時間軸** — vertical `divide-y --border` timeline; event-type chips
24	  (除權息/法說/財報/股東會/結算 — muted-tint pills); dates tabular-nums. Non-trading (events list).
25	- 目標價 rendered as an **attributed third-party datum**, never in `--up`/`--down` as if our call.
26	- States: shimmer card ghosts; coverage-gapped mid/small-cap → nothing, not empty scaffolding.
27	
28	## Governance
29	Analyst targets/ratings are **attributed third-party estimates**, never restated as our view (§7).
30	Coverage-gated (large-cap) → show nothing rather than empty scaffolding for mid/small-cap. Event
31	calendar is `public`, non-trading (an events list, not a signal).
