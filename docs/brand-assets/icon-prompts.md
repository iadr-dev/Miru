# 了然 / Miru — App Icon / Logo Mark: Image-Gen Prompt Directions

Standalone icon/mark exploration (not a full lockup with wordmark). Six distinct directions,
each a ready-to-use prompt for Midjourney/DALL-E/Stable-Diffusion-style image generation.
Source brand context: [`docs/brand.md`](../brand.md), color tokens in
[`docs/web/design-system.md`](../web/design-system.md) (`--accent:#2DD4BF` dark /
`#0E9E8E` light).

Shared constraints applied to every direction below:
- Single accent color only (teal-blue family), no gradients unless explicitly noted.
- Must reduce cleanly to a flat monochrome mark and stay legible at 32px.
- No literal bird illustration, no candlestick/bar-chart/arrow clichés, no AI-purple, no
  glossy 3D skeuomorphism, no circuit-board/neural-net motifs, no eagle/bull/bear.

---

## 1. "Aperture Iris" — the literal clarity-at-a-glance mark

**Rationale:** Most direct visualization of 一目了然 — an aperture/iris that is mid-resolving,
sharp and legible, standing for "seeing the market clearly." Reads as optics/analysis, not
finance-cliché, and is inherently favicon-friendly (apertures are a classic icon primitive).

**Prompt:**
```
Minimalist flat vector logo icon, a camera-aperture / iris diaphragm made of exactly 6 angular
blades arranged in a perfect circle, blades rendered as sharp-edged geometric shapes (not
photorealistic, not 3D), the aperture is drawn mid-close so a small precise circular negative-
space opening remains at the dead center — that open center is the focal point of the mark.
Single flat fill color #0E9E8E (teal-blue) for the blades on a pure white background (#FFFFFF),
no outlines, no gradients, no drop shadows, no bevels. Perfectly symmetrical, radial balance,
optically centered in a square canvas with generous padding (icon occupies ~70% of frame).
Line/shape weight: bold, confident, geometric — blade edges are straight or single-curve, no
organic wobble. Style: modern app-icon design, Swiss-grid precision, akin to a Linear or Raycard
icon aesthetic crossed with a Leica/Zeiss optics diagram. Vector flat illustration, clean
edges, no texture, no noise, no gloss. Square 1:1 composition, centered, isolated on white.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** photorealistic camera, glass lens reflections, 3D render, gradient,
drop shadow, bevel, chrome, bokeh photo background, arrow, chart, candlestick, bull, eagle,
bird illustration, text, watermark, mockup device frame.

**32px / monochrome survival:** the 6-blade ring with an open center reduces to a single dark
disc-with-hole silhouette at tiny sizes — recognizable as a lens/eye motif in pure black-on-white
or reversed white-on-black with zero detail loss.

## 2. "Resolving Stroke" — brushstroke revealing order from noise

**Rationale:** Visualizes "一目了然" as a single decisive gesture — one calligraphic stroke that
cuts through scattered, chaotic marks and leaves one clean resolved line — literalizing
"understanding emerging from noise" while carrying the Taiwanese ink-brush weight the brand
wants without looking ornamental/traditional.

**Prompt:**
```
Minimalist logo icon: a single confident brush-calligraphy stroke sweeping diagonally from lower-
left to upper-right across a square canvas, rendered with authentic ink-brush taper — thick,
pressured start, tapering to a fine dry-brush point at the end, like a single stroke of Chinese
calligraphy (書法) drawn with a flat pointed brush, but completely abstract, not a character.
Color: solid #2DD4BF teal-blue ink on a near-black background (#0A0A0A). Around the stroke,
scatter 4-5 tiny irregular ink specks/dots (representing noise/chaos) only on the side the
stroke originates from — the specks fade out and vanish as the stroke resolves, so the space the
stroke sweeps INTO is pure clean negative space, unbroken and empty. The contrast between the
cluttered origin corner and the calm resolved corner IS the composition. Flat 2D vector-style
ink rendering (no photographic brush texture, no paper grain), crisp anti-aliased edges,
generous margin, icon occupies about 65% of the square frame, optically balanced not dead-center.
Style: contemporary East Asian ink minimalism meets Swiss iconography, restrained, precise.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** actual kanji/hanzi character, calligraphy text, watercolor texture,
paper grain, photographic ink bleed, 3D, gradient mesh, arrow, chart line, candlestick, gaudy
color, multiple colors, ornate flourish, seal script, brush photograph.

**32px / monochrome favicon survival:** at small sizes the noise specks disappear naturally
(they're tiny already) leaving just the bold tapered stroke — a single solid dark diagonal
shape that stays legible and distinctive as a pure silhouette.

## 3. "Seal Mark" — 印章 (chop/stamp) proportions, TW craft direction

**Rationale:** This is the TW-craft-texture direction requested — borrows the squared, dense,
slightly-carved proportion logic of a traditional Taiwanese/Chinese name seal (印章) — a format
that reads as "authoritative, verified, trustworthy" (seals stamp official documents) — without
using any literal script, ornament, or red seal-paste color, keeping it modern/flat/vector.

**Prompt:**
```
Flat vector app icon shaped like a traditional East Asian name-seal (印章) stamp impression:
a perfect square with softly rounded corners (corner radius ~8% of icon size), the square's
edge rendered as a slightly uneven, hand-carved border of consistent medium thickness — not a
perfect machine-clean rectangle, but not sketchy either: think a single confident carving pass,
subtly irregular like stone-seal engraving but clean and intentional. Inside the square,
centered, a single abstract glyph-like mark that suggests an eye or a resolving lens shape
built from 2-3 straight/angular carved strokes (NOT a real Chinese character, purely abstract
geometric strokes evoking the WEIGHT and proportion of seal-script carving). Entire mark is a
single flat color #0E9E8E teal-blue on warm off-white paper background (#FAF7F0, a warm cream,
not pure white — evokes rice paper/xuan paper). No red ink, no gradients, no paper texture
photo-realism, no drop shadow — keep it flat vector with only the subtle intentional edge
irregularity described. Square 1:1 icon composition, mark fills ~75% of frame, dense and
confident like an actual seal impression, not spindly. Style: modern flat vector interpretation
of a 印章 seal-stamp, restrained and geometric, not ornate or antique-styled.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** red ink, red seal paste, actual readable hanzi/kanji character,
antique texture, cracked stone photo, 3D relief, drop shadow, gradient, ornate dragon/phoenix
motif, calligraphy brush texture, gold foil, vermillion, photorealistic stamp mockup.

**32px / monochrome favicon survival:** the rounded-square seal silhouette with a dense centered
mark behaves exactly like an app-icon "badge" shape already — at 32px the outer seal-square
reads as the icon's rounded-corner frame and the inner glyph reads as a single bold dark blob,
both scale gracefully to monochrome since there's no color-dependent detail.

## 4. "Horizon Eye" — market horizon line resolving into an eye

**Rationale:** Directly fuses the two brand metaphors — market data (a horizon/trend line) and
perception (見/see) — the moment a wavering line "settles" into the calm shape of an eye is a
visual pun for "market noise resolving into clear understanding," while deliberately avoiding
candlesticks/bars/arrows.

**Prompt:**
```
Minimalist geometric logo icon: a single continuous thin line enters from the left edge of a
square canvas as a jagged, irregular zigzag (representing volatile, noisy market movement —
5-6 sharp irregular peaks and troughs, hand-plotted feel but vector-clean), traveling left to
right the zigzag gradually smooths and its amplitude decreases, and by the right two-thirds of
the composition the line has resolved into one perfect, smooth, continuous almond/eye-shaped
contour (a simple lens/eye silhouette outline, no pupil detail, no eyelashes, purely geometric
almond curve) enclosing a small solid circular dot at its center representing a calm focal
point / pupil. Entire line and the eye shape and the center dot are a single continuous flat
stroke, uniform weight throughout (no taper), color #2DD4BF teal-blue, stroke weight
proportionally bold (about 6% of icon height) so it holds up small. Background: solid dark
navy-black (#0B0F12), plenty of negative space above and below the horizontal composition,
icon centered vertically, generous margin on all sides. Style: single-line minimalist logo
design (monoline icon), precise and calm, no serifs, no texture, no gradient.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** candlestick bars, bar chart, upward arrow, bull/bear silhouette,
realistic eye/eyeball, eyelash, iris texture, gradient fill, 3D, drop shadow, multiple colors,
photographic, human face, pupil reflection highlight.

**32px / monochrome favicon survival:** the design is already a single uniform-weight monoline
stroke with no fills or gradients — at 32px the jagged-to-smooth transition and eye shape remain
distinguishable as one clean gesture, and it renders perfectly as pure black/white line art.

## 5. "Cut Aperture Square" — pure geometric/abstract direction

**Rationale:** The purely abstract, non-representational direction — no eye, no bird, no brush;
just a confident geometric operation (a square with a precise diagonal "cut" revealing a circle
beneath) that abstractly encodes "cutting through complexity to find the clear, simple shape
underneath" — the most Linear/Raycast-restrained of the six, safest for a small square app icon.

**Prompt:**
```
Flat vector geometric app icon: a solid rounded square (corner radius ~18%, filling almost the
entire canvas, color #0E1319 near-black) has a single straight diagonal cut removed from its
upper-right corner at a precise 45-degree angle, the cut is clean and geometric (a straight
chamfer, not curved), and revealed within the negative space of that cut sits a perfect small
solid teal-blue circle (#2DD4BF), positioned so the circle is exactly half-revealed by the
diagonal cut and half still implied beneath the black square's edge — the circle reads as
"the clear thing that was always there, now revealed." No other elements: no lines, no texture,
no gradient, no second cut. The composition is asymmetric but balanced — the black square
occupies the majority of the frame, the teal circle is the sole accent and sole focal point,
positioned in the upper-right quadrant per the cut. Background outside the rounded square is
plain white (#FFFFFF) or transparent. Style: Bauhaus-influenced geometric minimalism, extreme
restraint, two shapes and two colors only, precise vector edges, no ornamentation whatsoever,
looks equally at home as a Linear/Raycast/Arc-browser-style app icon.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** gradient, 3D bevel, drop shadow, multiple colors, texture, pattern,
rounded organic blob, arrow, chart, letterform, mascot, gloss, skeuomorphic icon, photographic.

**32px / monochrome favicon survival:** two flat shapes, one diagonal cut, one circle — this is
the icon most likely to survive aggressive downscaling since it has zero fine detail; in
monochrome the teal circle simply becomes white/negative-space, and the silhouette (cut square)
remains instantly identifiable at 16-32px.

## 6. "Wing-Cut Lens" — subtle magpie-wing geometry inside a lens mark

**Rationale:** The most direct (but still abstracted) nod to 藍鵲 as inspiration rather than
subject — the negative-space shape cut into a circular "lens" mark echoes a single angular
magpie wingtip/feather silhouette, so anyone who knows the mascot recognizes the DNA, while
newcomers just read it as a sharp, confident abstract mark. Keeps mascot as subtext, not subject.

**Prompt:**
```
Minimalist flat vector logo icon: a perfect solid circle (representing a lens/eye/aperture),
color #2DD4BF teal-blue, filling ~80% of a square canvas, background pure off-white (#F7F5F0).
Cut into the circle from its lower-left edge is a single angular negative-space notch shaped
like one sharp, geometric bird-wingtip / feather silhouette — 3 straight-edged angular facets
suggesting a folded wing tip in flight, pointing diagonally toward the upper-right — rendered
purely as hard-edged geometric facets (NOT a soft feather illustration, NOT a literal bird, no
curves, no texture, must read as abstract geometry first and "wing" only as a secondary read).
The notch cuts all the way through to reveal the plain background color, creating a crisp
silhouette relationship between solid teal circle and the angular negative-space wing shape.
No outlines, no gradient, no drop shadow, no secondary colors. Perfectly clean vector edges,
optically centered with even margin on all sides. Style: modern flat geometric app-icon design,
restrained and confident, in the register of a premium fintech/productivity app icon (Linear /
Arc / Raycast tier), not playful or cartoonish.
```
**Negative prompt terms:** cartoon bird, cute mascot, feather texture, realistic wing, eagle,
bull, bear, gradient, 3D, drop shadow, bevel, multiple colors, outline stroke, photographic,
chart, arrow, candlestick, googly eye, beak, claws.

**32px / monochrome favicon survival:** a solid circle with one angular notch is one of the
simplest possible icon silhouettes — at 32px it reads instantly as a lens-with-a-cut, and in
monochrome the notch remains a clean background-colored bite out of a solid dark/light disc.

## How to use these

- Directions **1, 4, 6** lean into the eye/aperture/lens concept space (as requested, the
  favored space) with increasing abstraction (1 = literal aperture, 4 = eye+market fusion,
  6 = lens+wing-notch fusion).
- Direction **3** is the requested TW-craft/seal-stamp lean.
- Direction **2** is the brush-stroke-resolving-noise concept, also craft-adjacent but more
  gestural than direction 3's carved-seal feel.
- Direction **5** is the requested pure geometric/abstract lean, safest bet for small-size
  legibility and most "Linear/Raycast restraint."
- Recommend generating all six at 1:1 square aspect (`--ar 1:1` in Midjourney), then testing
  each winner by shrinking to 32px and converting to pure black/white before deciding — per the
  survival notes above, directions 5 and 6 are lowest-risk, directions 2 and 3 carry the most
  distinct brand personality if they render cleanly.
