20	## Visual design
21	The tab reads top→bottom as one story on a single `--surface` column (cards radius 16px, pad 20px,
22	`1px --border`, tinted shadow). Gauge visual = [../design-system.md §7.1](../design-system.md).
23	
24	- **多空 Score Gauge** — radial arc, **偏空→中性→偏多 sweeping green→neutral→red** (紅漲綠跌 convention);
25	  needle **springs to value on load, never re-animates on a quote tick**; reduced-motion → static at
26	  value. Big band label + numeric centered (`--font-num`, `--up`/`--down`-tinted by band).
27	- **Factor micro-bars** (動能/趨勢/估值/品質/籌碼) — horizontal bars beneath, each tinted + tabular
28	  value; low 估值 (value guard) → `--warn` tint (追高 caution). **Always shown** (never bare score).
29	- **Profile toggle** (swing/position) = segmented pill (active `--accent`); regime + over-extension
30	  caveat inline. 籌碼 factor's 分點 sub-signal masked for Free → "籌碼因子部分未解鎖" disclosure chip
31	  (composite recomputed, never silently degraded).
32	- **AI 智能摘要** — 市場定位 headline stays **sharp** (16px/1.7 CJK); factor-grounded narrative + SWOT
33	  4-quadrant grid. For Free, **SWOT body under 馬賽克** (backdrop-blur + diagonal texture + lock chip +
34	  升級 CTA); headline + quadrant frame stay sharp.
35	- Required footer: "AI-generated summary of data, not advice" + currentness `<Caveat>`.
36	
37	## Governance (strictest — §7)
38	Descriptive not prescriptive: 偏多/中性/偏空 describes what factors show, **never 買/賣/target/sizing**.
39	Score always ships its **factor breakdown + 描述** (show the work). SWOT "Opportunity" = factor
40	observation, never 進場點. "AI-generated summary of data, not advice" footer + currentness caveat mandatory.
